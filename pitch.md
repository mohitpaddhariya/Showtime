# Showtime – Pitch

Most people can show their work on a screen, but very few can turn that into a clean, professional demo video.

Founders, students, and small businesses all hit the same wall:
- they record long, rough screen videos for pitches, hackathons, or customer onboarding,
- editing is hard and time-consuming,
- so their product or project looks less impressive than it actually is.

**Showtime** is an AI tool that turns **any rough screen recording into a short, professional demo video** — automatically.

---

## How It Works

You just:
1. Record your screen (for a startup demo, hackathon project, class assignment, or product walkthrough).
2. Generate a professional voiceover using ElevenLabs (takes 2 minutes).
3. Upload both to Showtime.

Showtime then:
- cuts out the boring and irrelevant parts of the recording,
- syncs the voiceover precisely to what's happening on screen (with adaptive playback speed so it's never too fast),
- and gives you a tight, polished, easy-to-follow video — ready to share.

No editing skills needed. No video team. No back-and-forth.

---

## Who It's For

- **Startup founders** creating demo videos for YC, investors, and customers without a video team
- **Students** submitting clean project demos for hackathons and courses
- **Small businesses and support teams** turning quick screen recordings into reusable onboarding and help videos

---

## The Core Problem We Solve

Right now, if you have a polished voiceover and a raw screen recording, you still have to manually sync them — figuring out which part of the screen plays during which sentence, cutting dead time, and rendering it all. That's the part nobody wants to do.

Showtime automates exactly that:
- detects key moments in your screen recording (scene changes, clicks, scrolls),
- maps your voiceover to the right screen segments automatically,
- trims the dead time,
- and renders the final video for you.
